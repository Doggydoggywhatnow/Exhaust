# Exhaust 0.0.0

A robust and personable notepad that doesn't get in your way. Notes, journals, knowledge bases and project plans, linked together.

## Values:

- **Customizable, but not overwhelmingly**: Exhaust offers settings to change the workflow of the notepad to fit your needs. You can even set the default size of a new exhaust window. Despite this, it is not overloaded with features. We include a robust kit of text editing features, some quality of life things, and allow you to control which features you want and where you want them. As well as what you want everything included to look like.
- **Aesthetic**: The design of Exhaust is meant to be simple and pleasant to the eye. It is inspired by the design of Midori MD notebooks.
- **Simple**: Exhaust provides a simple environment in which to use complex features. And where features present in other apps do not go directly towards text, we will drop those features.
- **Small**: The entire program will be minimal in size.
- **Private**: What you write is your business. Simple as.

## Features

- **Links**: Connect ideas, people, places, books and anything else with `[[wikilinks]]`. Backlinks show what points at the note you are reading. Build your own personal Wikipedia.
- **Graph**: See the relationships between your notes as an interactive graph, plus a local graph for the open note. Find hidden patterns in your thinking.
- **Canvas**: An infinite space to research, brainstorm, diagram and lay out ideas, with notes placed on it.
- **Plugins**: Tailor Exhaust to your workflow through an open API, themes and scripts.
- **Sync** *(optional, later)*: Access your notes on any device, end-to-end encrypted. Version history per note, and fine-grained control over which files and settings sync to which devices.
- **Collaboration** *(optional, later)*: Shared vaults, invited by email, with owner and editor roles, without exposing the rest of your private data.
- **Publish** *(optional, later)*: Turn notes into an online wiki, knowledge base, documentation or digital garden, with themes, custom domains and password protection.

Sync, collaboration and publish are off by default. Exhaust makes no network requests unless you turn one on.
