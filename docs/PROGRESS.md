Read this first when resuming. It tracks the sub-slices of [`0.1.0.md`](0.1.0.md) in build order. Update it in the same commit as each sub-slice.

**Status values:** `not started`, `in progress`, `done` (tests pass locally and CI is green on Linux, Windows and macOS), `done, CI pending` (committed and pushed, CI not yet confirmed green on all three).

**Rules:** one sub-slice at a time; commit and push to `dev` after each; CI green on all three OSes before the next; never push to `main`; never skip, loosen or delete a test. **Checkpoints, stop and report for review:** after 1b-iv (first typing build), after 3b (first live-preview build), after 4b-iv (first usable build).

the CI fuzz job now runs both for 60 seconds. **Bugs the tests found:** an uninitialized table-cell alignment; a paragraph that ended at an indented line that could not start a table, and at a line that matched a higher-priority block (the block parser and the paragraph terminator now share one priority order); a list item swallowing its next sibling as lazy continuation (sibling markers end an item whatever their number); front matter and the first block not being memoryless; a non-idempotent depth limit in the HTML cleaner. **Honest gaps:** reference-style links (`[a][b]`, `[b]: url`) are not tokenized because Obsidian does not use them; HTML entities in Markdown text are not tokenized; setext headings inside lists follow the same rules as at the top; a block that is longer than 4 GB is not supported. **TODO-verify:** how Obsidian treats a few edge cases (a tag directly after `(`, `$` followed by a digit) is implemented from its documented rules, not from its source. |
